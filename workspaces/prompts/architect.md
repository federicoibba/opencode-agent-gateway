name: Design Architecture
description: Design or review a system against the architecture skill.
tags: [architecture, design]
---

Design or review the system I describe, using the `architecture` skill:

1. Restate the requirements and non-functional constraints.
2. Propose boundaries and the direction of dependencies.
3. Give the trade-offs: options, pros/cons and your recommendation.
4. Define interfaces with the `api-design` skill where relevant.
5. Record consequential decisions with the `adr` skill.

State assumptions and the main risks. Prefer evolving what exists over a rewrite.
