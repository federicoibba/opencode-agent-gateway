Explore the area I describe and report, using the `planning` skill's tracing
method:

- **Entry points** — how the feature is triggered.
- **Execution flow** — the call chain, branches and async boundaries.
- **Key files** — a table of file, role and importance.
- **Dependencies** — external and internal.
- **Recommendations** — what to follow, reuse or avoid.

Flag anything you could not verify from the code you can see.
