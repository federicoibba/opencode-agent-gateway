Review the performance of what I share, measurement first:

- **Web** — load `performance-web`: Core Web Vitals, bundle, rendering, hydration.
- **Backend** — load `performance-backend`: latency versus throughput, allocations,
  concurrency, queries and caching.

Rank findings by impact and give the smallest change that moves the metric. Say
what you could not measure.
